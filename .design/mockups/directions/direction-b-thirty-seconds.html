why

Why we do not show her a calendar

Every other diary opens on a month grid. That is the honest thing to build if you do not know who is looking at it: pick a date, pick a time, hope something is free, start again if it is not.

But you do know her. She comes every eight weeks, she has a medium doodle who takes ninety minutes, and she wants the first Thursday morning you have got. A grid makes her work that out. We already know it, so we say it.

The grid still exists. It is behind “another time”, which is the quietest control on the page, because the people who need it are the exception and not the rule.

Where a grid still wins

A client with one specific afternoon free in the next month is better served by seeing the whole month at once. So is anyone booking around a holiday.

They get there in one tap, and we would rather that tap existed than pretend a proposal suits everybody.
