1Registration flow — the diagnosis, then two ways to make five screens one flow

Read the review card first, then compare 1a (everything on the wizard chrome of screens 2–5) against 1b (everything on the two-pane chrome of screen 1). Colour, type and components are taken from your live screens — nothing is restyled here, only made consistent.

UI REVIEW · WHY SCREEN 1 READS AS A DIFFERENT PRODUCT
1. Two layout systems

Screen 1 is a 50/50 split with a stepper rail. Screens 2–5 are a centred column with a top progress bar and a breadcrumb. Nothing carries over between them, so step 1 → 2 feels like leaving the app.

2. The step count disagrees with itself

Screen 1 lists five steps and marks itself as 1. Screen 2 then says “Step 1 of 4”. The user completes a step and the counter goes backwards. Pick one denominator — five — and keep it.

3. Navigation moves

Screen 1's primary action is full-width inside the form; 2–5 park it in a fixed bottom bar on the right. The commit target jumps ~500px between the first two screens.

4. Type scale and weight jump

“Set up your business” is light and roughly 44px; “Tell us about the business” is bold and roughly 56px. Same level in the hierarchy, two different treatments.

5. Measure is uncontrolled

Inputs are ~640px on screen 1 and ~1060px on 2–5. A postcode field a metre wide tells the user nothing about the answer expected. Cap fields at ~520px and size short ones to content.

6. Brand disappears after step 1

The wordmark shows only on screen 1; afterwards it is an uppercase text breadcrumb. Keep the mark in the flow chrome — this is the sign-up, the highest-anxiety moment in the product.

7. Half the viewport does no work

Screen 1's right pane holds a five-item list in ~1200px of space, with a full-height divider. Either the rail earns its width on every screen (1b) or it goes (1a).

8. Unthemed controls and copy bugs

Screen 2's checkbox is the native control with the browser's blue focus ring. Screen 1's billing line switches into mono mid-sentence (“£29 a month”). Screen 4's helper cites a 90 minute slot while the field says 60.

RECOMMENDED

Move screen 1 onto the wizard chrome (1a). Progress, back-navigation and the commit bar are already right on four of five screens, and account creation is genuinely step 1 of 5 — not a preamble. The two-pane rail in 1b is the better answer only if you want persistent context (the stepper, a live preview) visible on every step, and are willing to rebuild four screens to get it.

1aUnified wizard — screen 1 adopts the chrome of 2–5. Five segments, persistent wordmark, one commit bar, measure capped at 520px.
